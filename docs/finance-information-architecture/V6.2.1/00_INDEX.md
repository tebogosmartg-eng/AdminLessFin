## Board Decision (permanent)

**Live Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and Ratio Analysis belong under Accounting (Accounting Reports) — not under Reports, and not under Financial Statements Workspace.**

| Track | Home | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| Live books presentation | **Accounting → Accounting Reports** | Live TB, IS, BS, CF, Ratios |
| Statutory preparation | **Financial Statements Workspace** | Close, EFRE Statements/Disclosures/Publication |
| Enterprise-wide reporting | **Reports** | Executive + cross-domain enterprise reports only |

Rationale: calculation ownership is Accounting; primary users are accountants/controllers; TB is a control artefact of the books; Reports must not become a second home for the same statements; statutory packs remain exclusively under Financial Statements Workspace; future frameworks attach to Workspace/EFRE only.

**V6.2.0 refinement:** Live FS presentation ownership formerly assigned to Operational Reports is **superseded** by this pack for those five artefacts.
