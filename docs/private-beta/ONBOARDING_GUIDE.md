# AdminLess Fin — Private Beta Onboarding Guide

**Audience:** Company owners and finance professionals  
**Beta model:** Single-owner companies, white-glove support

---

## Overview

AdminLess Fin requires a validated **accounting foundation** before operational modules unlock. This guide walks you from sign-up to your first financial statements.

### The five-stage journey

1. Create your account  
2. Create your company  
3. Complete Accounting Setup (six steps)  
4. Add customers and suppliers  
5. Record your first transactions  

In-app guide: **Help → Onboarding Guide** (`/onboarding-guide`)

---

## Stage 1 — Account

1. Visit the welcome page and click **Get started** (or go to `/auth`).
2. Use the **Sign up** tab to create an account with email and password.
3. Verify your email if your administrator has enabled email confirmation.
4. Sign in — you will be prompted to create a company if you do not have one.

---

## Stage 2 — Company

1. Enter your **company name** (e.g. "ACME Inc.").
2. Click **Create company & continue**.
3. You are redirected to **Accounting Setup** automatically.

---

## Stage 3 — Accounting Setup

Open **Accounting Setup** from the dashboard progress card or sidebar.

| Step | Action | Why it matters |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Financial Calendar | Set FY end date and save | Transactions must fall in an open financial year |
| Chart of Accounts | **Generate Standard** (recommended) | Defines how all money is classified |
| Tax | Add at least one rate (e.g. VAT 15%) | Required for invoice and bill posting |
| Banking | Add bank account or **Skip for now** | Links cash to the ledger |
| Opening Balances | **Confirm zero** for new companies | Establishes starting position |
| Validation | Review all checks pass | Unlocks invoicing, journals, and reports |

When complete, the dashboard shows **Accounting Ready**.

---

## Stage 4 — Master data

After Accounting Ready:

- **Customers** — Sales → Customers → New Customer  
- **Suppliers** — Purchasing → Suppliers → New Supplier  

These can be added before Accounting Ready, but invoicing requires both setup completion and a customer record.
